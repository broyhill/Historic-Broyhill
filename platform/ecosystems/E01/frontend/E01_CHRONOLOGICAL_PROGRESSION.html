First presentation
Original Concept Presentation — "Donor Intelligence Mapping" CONCEPTION
The very first documented version of what became E01. Presented as part of the BroyhillGOP Final Presentation deck. At this point it had no code, no schema, no ecosystem number — just a concept: use AI to map donor propensity for NC Republican candidates.
The idea was simple: stop reaching out to everyone the same way. Use AI to predict who will give, how much, and on which issue — then contact them exactly that way.

Nov 6, 2025
Full specification
Full Donor Intelligence Specification — "North Carolina Republican Donor Intelligence System" SPEC
The founding document of E01. 111KB, 24 pages. Everything E01 was designed to be. This is the original bible — written before a single line of code existed.
WHAT IT SAID E01 WOULD DO: Predict donation propensity for 150,000 NC GOP donors using XGBoost machine learning trained on 6 NC-specific feature categories.
The 6 Feature Categories (with weights):
1. Religious Identity (35%) — 6 categories: Evangelical Protestant (2.1x), LDS (2.5x), Mainline Protestant (1.4x), Catholic (1.3x), Jewish (0.7x), Non-Religious (0.6x). Each tracked by denomination with propensity multipliers.
2. NC University Alumni Loyalty (25%) — Duke (1.8x), Wake Forest (1.9x), UNC (1.2x), NC State (1.7x), private religious colleges (2.0x+). ACC rivalry intelligence used for donor segmentation.
3. Professional & Industry Affiliation (20%) — Banking/Finance Charlotte (1.8x), Textile/Manufacturing (2.2x), Agriculture (2.7x), Healthcare (1.4x), Real Estate (2.0x), Legal/Federalist Society (2.3x).
4. Military Service (15%) — Veterans (1.9x), combat veterans (2.4x), 82nd Airborne/Special Forces (2.8x). Fort Liberty, Camp Lejeune, Seymour Johnson AFB as key NC bases.
5. Civic & Community Leadership (10%) — Chamber of Commerce, Rotary (337 clubs in NC), Farm Bureau (580K members). Propensity multiplier: 1.6x.
6. Past Donation History (25%) — SINGLE MOST PREDICTIVE FEATURE. RFM model: 1 donation in past year = 2.1x; 3+ donations = 4.7x; $5,000+ total = 5.8x.
ML Model Architecture:
XGBoost Multi-Output Regression. 4 predicted outputs per donor: (1) Probability of donation next 90 days (0–100%), (2) Expected donation amount ($10–$10K+), (3) Donation frequency per year, (4) Channel preference (Email/Mail/Event/Phone/Text).
Training set: 50,000 NC donors with known outcomes. Performance targets: 82% precision, 67% recall, 4.2x ROI vs. random outreach.
Feature vector: 18 dimensions including religious category, university tier, industry, military status, civic org count, donation history, home value, age, county, urban/rural score, church attendance, social media conservative score, yard sign detected.

4-Tier Outreach Strategy:
Tier 1 (Score 90–100, ~3,000 donors): Candidate phone call, ask $2,500–$10,000. Tier 2 (75–89, ~15,000): Staff call + personalized email, ask $500–$2,500. Tier 3 (60–74, ~35,000): Mass personalized email, ask $100–$500. Tier 4 (<60, ~97,000): Generic email/text, ask $25–$100.
Innovative additions in original spec:
Computer vision yard sign detection: YOLO model trained on NC political yard signs. Google Street View scan of all 150K donor addresses. Yard sign detected = +15 propensity score.
OpenAI deep research integration: o4-mini deep research for top 1,000 scored donors. 8 research dimensions per donor including church membership, university athletic donor status, Federalist Society membership.
PostgresML implementation: Full SQL training code for XGBoost model directly in Postgres. Score all 150K donors with SQL UPDATE. Top 10K prospects query.
NC Conservative Org Directory: 15+ organizations with FEC IDs, Guidestar links, donor lookup URLs (John Locke Foundation, NC Values Coalition, AFP, Heritage Foundation, ADF, Samaritan's Purse).
